# Kingsrun

A five-suit rummy card game with a rotating wild card, played over 11 rounds.
Play against 1–3 computer opponents or pass and play with 2–4 people on one phone.

Installs to your home screen and works offline.

- **iPhone:** open the site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
- **Android:** open the site in Chrome → ⋮ → Install app

Fonts: Young Serif, Figtree and IBM Plex Mono, under the SIL Open Font License (see `FONTS-LICENSE.txt`).
